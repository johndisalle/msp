2. Description of Service

AbideJourney is a faith-based devotional app that provides daily Scripture readings, devotionals, reflection prompts, prayer tools, and action steps organized into themed 40-day journeys. The App includes features such as:

  • Themed devotional journeys (free and premium)
  • AI-generated custom journeys personalized to your situation
  • Listen Mode with AI-narrated devotionals and ambient soundscapes
  • Community Prayer Wall for sharing and praying for requests
  • Community Testimony Wall for sharing faith stories
  • Personal Prayer Wall for private prayer tracking
  • Scripture Memory flashcard practice
  • Breathing meditation exercises with Scripture
  • God Moments journal for recording spiritual experiences
  • Find a Church location-based church finder
  • Seasonal Journeys (Advent, Lent, Holy Week, and more)
  • Couples Journey and Family Journey options
  • Faith Wrapped Year in Review
  • Journal with voice transcription
