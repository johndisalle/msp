Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 9, 2026

1. Introduction

AbideJourney ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices regarding your data.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

AbideJourney stores the following data locally on your device and, if enabled, synced via iCloud:

  • Journey progress: Your current day, completed days, action steps, and bookmarks
  • Journal entries: Text and voice-transcribed reflections you write during your journey
  • Personal prayer requests: Prayers you submit to your private Prayer Wall, including answered prayer notes
  • God Moments: Spiritual experiences you record
  • Scripture memory verses: Verses you save for memorization practice
  • Preferences and settings: Theme, notification times, Bible translation, appearance mode

This data is stored using Apple's SwiftData framework and may sync across your devices via iCloud if you have iCloud enabled. We do not have access to your iCloud data.

3. Information Sent to Our Servers

Certain features require communication with our secure servers (hosted on Google Cloud via Firebase). When you use these features, the following data is transmitted:

Community Prayer Wall & Testimony Wall

When you choose to share a prayer request or testimony with the community, the following is sent to and stored in our database (Google Cloud Firestore):

  • The text of your prayer request or testimony
  • The display name you provide (or "Anonymous" if you choose)
  • The category you select
  • A device identifier used to associate your submissions (not your Apple ID or personal identity)

Community posts are visible to all users of the App. You choose whether to share with the community — your private Prayer Wall remains on-device only. We reserve the right to remove community content that violates our guidelines.

Custom AI Journey Generation

When you create a custom journey, the description you provide is sent to our server, which forwards it to an AI service (Anthropic Claude API) to generate personalized devotional content. We do not store your description after the journey is generated. The AI-generated journey content is returned to your device and stored locally.

Listen Mode (AI Audio Narration)

When you use Listen Mode, the text of the day's devotional content is sent to our server, which forwards it to a text-to-speech service (ElevenLabs) to generate audio narration. The audio is returned to your device and cached locally. We do not store the text or audio on our servers after delivery.

Device Identifier & Rate Limiting

We use your device's vendor identifier (a non-personal, device-specific ID provided by Apple) solely for rate limiting to prevent abuse of our services. This identifier is not linked to your Apple ID, name, email, or any personal information. Rate limit records are stored temporarily and automatically expire.

4. Location Data

The Find a Church feature requests your location to search for nearby churches using Apple's MapKit. Your location is:

  • Used only at the moment of search to find churches near you
  • Processed entirely on your device and through Apple's mapping services
  • Never stored, transmitted to our servers, or shared with third parties

Location access is optional. The app functions fully without it. You can revoke location permission at any time in your device Settings.

5. Voice and Audio Data

AbideJourney uses two audio-related features:

  • Voice Journaling (Premium): Uses your device's microphone and Apple's Speech Recognition to transcribe spoken reflections into text journal entries. Audio is processed on-device by Apple's speech framework and is not recorded, stored, or transmitted by AbideJourney.
  • Listen Mode (Premium): Generates professional AI-narrated audio of devotional content. Devotional text is sent to our server for audio generation (see Section 3). The resulting audio is cached on your device for offline playback.

Microphone and speech recognition permissions are requested only when you use Voice Journaling and can be revoked at any time in your device Settings.

6. Apple Sign-In

If you choose to sign in with Apple, we may receive your name and email address (depending on your Apple ID sharing preferences). This information is stored locally on your device to personalize your profile and is not transmitted to external servers.

7. Subscription Information

Subscription purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not collect, store, or have access to your payment information, Apple ID, or billing details. Apple's privacy policy governs the handling of your purchase data.

8. User-Generated Content

Content you create within the app falls into two categories:

  • Private content (journal entries, personal prayer requests, God Moments, Scripture memory) is stored locally on your device and synced via iCloud if enabled. This content is not accessible to us or any third party.
  • Community content (prayers and testimonies you choose to share with the community) is stored on our servers and visible to other users. You can choose to post anonymously.

Seed testimonies displayed in the Testimony Wall are sample stories included with the app and are not from real users.

9. Third-Party Services

AbideJourney uses the following third-party services on the server side only:

  • Google Cloud / Firebase: Hosts our server functions and community data (Firestore database)
  • Anthropic (Claude API): Generates personalized AI devotional content for custom journeys
  • ElevenLabs: Generates AI-narrated audio for Listen Mode

These services process data only as needed to provide their respective features. We do not share personal information with these services — only devotional text content and anonymous device identifiers for rate limiting.

10. Analytics

AbideJourney does not use third-party analytics or tracking services. We do not collect usage statistics or behavioral data. Basic app functionality signals (such as journey completion events) are processed locally on-device only.

11. Health and Wellness

The Breathe with Scripture feature provides guided breathing exercises for spiritual reflection. This feature does not collect, store, or transmit any health or fitness data. No data from this feature is shared with Apple Health or any external service.

12. Children's Privacy

AbideJourney includes a Family Journey feature that provides age-appropriate devotional content for children, teens, and adults. This feature:

  • Does not collect personal information from children
  • Stores family member names locally on the parent's device only
  • Does not require children to create accounts or submit personal data
  • Is designed to be set up and supervised by a parent or guardian

Children should not use community features (Prayer Wall, Testimony Wall) without parental supervision. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us immediately.

13. Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect:

  • Advertising or tracking identifiers
  • Browsing history or cross-app tracking data
  • Contacts, photos, or camera data
  • Biometric data
  • Your Apple ID, email address, or payment information on our servers

14. Data Deletion

You can delete all your local app data at any time by going to Settings > Delete All Data & Reset within the app. This permanently removes all locally stored data. If iCloud sync is enabled, data will also be removed from iCloud. Community content you have posted (prayers and testimonies shared publicly) may remain visible to other users. To request removal of community content, please contact us.

15. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
