Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 8, 2026

1. Introduction

AbideJourney ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices regarding your data.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

AbideJourney stores the following data locally on your device and, if enabled, synced via iCloud:

  • Journey progress: Your current day, completed days, action steps, and bookmarks
  • Journal entries: Text and voice-transcribed reflections you write during your journey
  • Prayer requests: Prayers you submit to the Prayer Wall, including answered prayer notes
  • Testimonies: Stories you share on the Testimony Wall
  • God Moments: Spiritual experiences you record
  • Scripture memory verses: Verses you save for memorization practice
  • Preferences and settings: Theme, notification times, Bible translation, appearance mode

This data is stored using Apple's SwiftData framework and may sync across your devices via iCloud if you have iCloud enabled. We do not have access to your iCloud data.

3. Location Data

The Find a Church feature requests your location to search for nearby churches using Apple's MapKit. Your location is:

  • Used only at the moment of search to find churches near you
  • Processed entirely on your device and through Apple's mapping services
  • Never stored, transmitted to our servers, or shared with third parties

Location access is optional. The app functions fully without it. You can revoke location permission at any time in your device Settings.

4. Voice and Audio Data

AbideJourney uses two audio-related features:

  • Voice Journaling (Premium): Uses your device's microphone and Apple's Speech Recognition to transcribe spoken reflections into text journal entries. Audio is processed on-device by Apple's speech framework and is not recorded, stored, or transmitted by AbideJourney.
  • Listen Mode (Premium): Uses Apple's text-to-speech (AVSpeechSynthesizer) to read devotionals aloud with ambient soundscapes. All audio is generated on-device. No data is sent externally.

Microphone and speech recognition permissions are requested only when you use Voice Journaling and can be revoked at any time in your device Settings.

5. Apple Sign-In

If you choose to sign in with Apple, we may receive your name and email address (depending on your Apple ID sharing preferences). This information is stored locally on your device to personalize your profile and is not transmitted to external servers.

6. Subscription Information

Subscription purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not collect, store, or have access to your payment information, Apple ID, or billing details. Apple's privacy policy governs the handling of your purchase data.

7. User-Generated Content

Content you create within the app (journal entries, prayer requests, testimonies, God Moments) is stored locally on your device and synced via iCloud if enabled. This content is:

  • Under your full control — you can delete it at any time
  • Not accessible to us or any third party
  • Not used for advertising, analytics, or any purpose beyond your personal use

Seed testimonies displayed in the Testimony Wall are sample stories included with the app and are not from real users.

8. Analytics

AbideJourney does not use third-party analytics or tracking services. We do not collect usage statistics, device identifiers, or behavioral data. Basic app functionality signals (such as journey completion events) are processed locally on-device only.

9. Health and Wellness

The Breathe with Scripture feature provides guided breathing exercises for spiritual reflection. This feature does not collect, store, or transmit any health or fitness data. No data from this feature is shared with Apple Health or any external service.

10. Children's Privacy

AbideJourney includes a Family Journey feature that provides age-appropriate devotional content for children, teens, and adults. This feature:

  • Does not collect personal information from children
  • Stores family member names locally on the parent's device only
  • Does not require children to create accounts or submit personal data
  • Is designed to be set up and supervised by a parent or guardian

We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us immediately.

11. Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect:

  • Advertising or tracking identifiers
  • Browsing history or cross-app tracking data
  • Contacts, photos, or camera data
  • Biometric data

12. Data Deletion

You can delete all your app data at any time by going to Settings > Delete All Data & Reset within the app. This permanently removes all locally stored data. If iCloud sync is enabled, data will also be removed from iCloud.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
