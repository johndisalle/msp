Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start a journey?

After downloading AbideJourney, complete the brief onboarding quiz and select a theme that resonates with you. Each day includes Scripture, a devotional, reflection prompts, and action steps to guide your walk with God.

How many days can I complete per day?

Free users can complete 1 day per calendar day. Premium members can complete up to 3 days per calendar day.

What does Premium include?

  • Listen Mode — Professional AI-narrated devotionals with ambient soundscapes (rain, piano, nature, ocean, worship). Choose between male and female narrators.
  • Custom AI Journeys — Describe what you're going through and AI builds a personalized 40-day devotional journey just for you
  • Community Posting — Share prayer requests and testimonies with the global community of believers
  • All journey themes — 13+ deep-dive themes including Overcoming Anxiety, Walking Through Grief, and more
  • Couples Journey — Walk through 40 days of spiritual growth with your partner
  • Family Journey — Age-appropriate devotional content for your whole family
  • Seasonal Journeys — Advent, Lent, Holy Week, and seasonal faith experiences
  • Voice Journaling — Speak your reflections instead of typing
  • Accountability Partners — Invite friends to encourage each other
  • Faith Map & Year in Review — Visualize your spiritual growth over time
  • Gift a Journey — Share a premium journey with someone you love
  • Export Journals — Save your reflections as beautiful PDFs
  • 3 days per calendar day — Move through journeys at your own pace

How does Listen Mode work?

Listen Mode (Premium) reads your daily devotional aloud using professional AI-generated narration. You can choose between two voice options — a female narrator (Sarah) or a male narrator (David). You can also add ambient soundscapes like rain, gentle piano, nature sounds, ocean waves, or soft worship music to create a peaceful listening experience. Audio is generated on first listen and cached on your device for offline playback.

What are Custom AI Journeys?

Custom AI Journeys (Premium) let you describe your specific situation — what you're going through, what you're struggling with, or what area you want to grow in — and AI generates a complete, personalized 40-day devotional journey with unique Scripture selections, devotionals, prayers, reflection prompts, and action steps tailored to your needs. You can generate up to 3 custom journeys per day.

What is the Prayer Wall?

The Prayer Wall has two sections:

  • My Prayers — Your private prayer requests stored on your device. Submit requests, track how many times you've prayed, and celebrate when God answers.
  • Community — Prayer requests shared by believers around the world. Read others' prayers, tap "Pray" to let them know you're praying, and share your own requests with the community. You can choose to post anonymously.

What is the Testimony Wall?

The Testimony Wall features stories of how God is working in people's lives — stories of salvation, healing, deliverance, and faith. It has two sections:

  • My Stories — Your personal testimonies and seed stories for inspiration
  • Community — Real testimonies shared by believers worldwide. Read, pray for, and be encouraged by how God is moving.

When you submit a testimony, you can choose to share it with the community to encourage other believers.

What are Seasonal Journeys?

Seasonal Journeys are special devotional experiences tied to the church calendar. They include Advent (25 days), Lent (40 days), Holy Week (7 days), New Year Faith Reset (21 days), Summer of Faith (30 days), and Spring Renewal (21 days). These automatically appear when the season arrives.

How does Find a Church work?

The Find a Church feature uses your location to discover churches near you. You can filter by denomination, view results on a map or list, get directions, call, or visit church websites. Your location is only used at the moment of search and is never stored.

What is Breathe with Scripture?

Breathe with Scripture is a guided breathing meditation that pairs calming breath exercises with Scripture. Choose from different breathing patterns and meditate on God's Word as you breathe. Great for moments of anxiety or when you need to center yourself in God's presence.

What are God Moments?

God Moments is a photo and caption journal where you capture moments when you see God at work in your daily life. It's a visual record of His faithfulness — answered prayers, unexpected blessings, moments of peace, or anything that reminds you of His presence.

How does Family Journey work?

Family Journey (Premium) lets you create a devotional experience for your family. Add family members, choose an age-appropriate theme, and get daily content tailored for children, teens, and adults — including family challenges and prayers.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your journal entries, personal prayers, God Moments, and journey progress are stored locally on your device (and synced via iCloud if enabled). We cannot see your private data. Community content (prayers and testimonies you choose to share) is visible to other users — you control what you share and can post anonymously. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time through your device's Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period.

Can I restore my purchases on a new device?

Yes. When you open AbideJourney on a new device signed into the same Apple ID, your premium status will be automatically restored. If it doesn't restore immediately, go to the Premium screen and tap "Restore Purchases."

How do I delete my data?

Go to Settings > Delete All Data & Reset within the app. This permanently removes all your local data including journeys, journal entries, prayers, and settings. Community content you've shared (public prayers and testimonies) may remain visible. To request removal of community content, please contact us.

I'm having trouble with the app. What should I do?

Try these steps:

  • Close and reopen the app
  • Restart your device
  • Check that you're running the latest version from the App Store
  • If using iCloud sync, ensure iCloud is enabled in Settings
  • For Listen Mode or Custom Journey issues, check your internet connection

If the issue persists, please contact us using the information below.
